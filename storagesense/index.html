Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Last updated: June 2026

Storage Sense does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Your photos and contacts are analyzed entirely on your device and never leave it.

What We Access

Storage Sense reads the following only on your device, to find duplicates and free up space:

  • Photos & videos — to detect exact duplicates, near-duplicates (compressed re-saves), similar shots, and large videos. Image content is processed locally to compute on-device fingerprints; it is never uploaded.
  • Contacts — to find and merge entries that share a phone number, email, or name. Contact data is never uploaded.
  • Device storage statistics — total and available space, to show your usage.

None of this information is ever transmitted off your device.

What We Store

The following is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your settings (appearance, app icon, scan thresholds, preferences)
  • Scan results and history (references to items on your device)
  • Premium purchase status (verified locally via StoreKit)

No Tracking, No Servers

Storage Sense has no analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting tools, or tracking frameworks. There are no user accounts and no servers associated with this app. The app makes no network requests of its own.

In-App Purchase

Storage Sense offers an optional one-time upgrade, Storage Sense Pro ($19.99). All purchase processing is handled by Apple's StoreKit framework. Storage Sense does not receive, store, or process any payment information. Your purchase is verified locally on-device and supports Family Sharing.

Deleting Items

When you delete photos or videos, they follow your device's normal removal flow and can be recovered from the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album for a limited time. Merging contacts updates your contacts directly through Apple's Contacts framework.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes Storage Sense's stored settings and scan history. You can also clear it anytime via Settings → Delete All Data; this does not affect your photos or contacts.

Children's Privacy

Storage Sense does not collect data from anyone. Since no data leaves the device, there are no age-based restrictions on data handling.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
