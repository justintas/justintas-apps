Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Last updated: May 2026

DailyThreeX does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All habits and check-in records stay locally on your device.

What We Store

DailyThreeX does not collect any personal information. The following data is stored locally on your device only:

  • Habit names, icons, and color choices
  • Daily check-in history (dates you completed each habit)
  • Streak data derived from your check-in history
  • App appearance preference (System / Light / Dark)
  • Premium purchase status (verified locally via StoreKit)

None of this data is ever transmitted off your device.

No Internet Connection Required

DailyThreeX is fully offline. The app does not make any network requests — not for analytics, not for crash reporting, and not for any other purpose. An internet connection is only used by StoreKit to process the optional one-time premium upgrade, which is handled entirely by Apple.

In-App Purchase

DailyThreeX offers an optional one-time premium upgrade ($4.99). All purchase processing is handled by Apple's StoreKit framework. DailyThreeX does not receive, store, or process any payment information. Your purchase receipt is verified locally on-device.

Third-Party Services

DailyThreeX does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting tools, or tracking frameworks. There are no user accounts and no servers associated with this app.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. Deleting the app permanently removes all stored data. You can also manually delete any habit or reset all data at any time via Settings → Reset All Data.

iCloud (Premium Feature)

Premium users can optionally enable iCloud Sync. If enabled, your habit data is synced across your personal Apple devices via your iCloud account, governed entirely by Apple's iCloud privacy policy. DailyThreeX never accesses or stores your iCloud credentials.

Children's Privacy

DailyThreeX does not collect data from anyone. Since no data leaves the device, there are no age-based restrictions on data handling.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
