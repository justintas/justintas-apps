Gyro Dash
Tilt-Controlled Arcade Racer
Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Last updated: May 2026

Gyro Dash does not collect any personal data itself. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and never transmitted anywhere.

What the App Stores Locally

The following data is saved on your device only and never uploaded:

  • High score, longest run, and local leaderboard
  • Coin balance and owned vehicles
  • Weapon upgrade levels
  • Game settings (tilt sensitivity, sound, music, haptics)
  • In-app purchase status (No Ads, Double Coins, VIP)

Deleting the app removes all of this data permanently.

Gyroscope & Motion

Gyro Dash uses your device's gyroscope and accelerometer (CoreMotion) to steer the car. Motion data is used in real-time only and is never stored, logged, or transmitted.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Gyro Dash shows ads powered by Google AdMob for free players. AdMob may collect the following to serve relevant ads:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

You can limit ad tracking at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads will still appear but will be less personalised.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchasing the No Ads or VIP pack removes all advertising entirely.

In-App Purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. Gyro Dash never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/privacy.

No Accounts, No Servers

Gyro Dash has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend servers. The game runs entirely on your device.

Children's Privacy

Gyro Dash is not directed at children under 13. The app displays ads through Google AdMob. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
