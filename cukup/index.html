Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Last updated: May 2026

Cukup does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All budget calculations and expense records stay locally on your device.

What We Collect

Cukup does not collect any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Income sources, frequencies, and amounts
  • Fixed commitments (bills, savings goals, investments)
  • Subscription logs with renewal dates and amounts
  • Expense history with categories, amounts, dates, and notes
  • Budget period reset day preference
  • Home currency selection
  • App preferences (theme, appearance settings)

None of this data is ever transmitted off your device.

Currency Exchange Rates

Cukup requests internet access for one optional feature only: fetching live currency exchange rates from frankfurter.app. This request contains no personal information — only a request for publicly available exchange rate data.

Exchange rates are cached locally for 4 hours to minimize network requests. All currency conversions happen on your device. You can use Cukup entirely offline; exchange rates are only needed if you log expenses in multiple currencies.

Third-Party Services

Cukup does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or tracking frameworks. There are no user accounts, no servers, and no in-app purchases that transmit personal data.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework on your device. Deleting the app permanently removes all stored data. You can also manually delete any expense, subscription, or income source at any time within the app.

Notifications

Cukup Pro includes optional local reminders that notify you 3 days before a subscription renews. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device — no notification data is sent to any server.

Children's Privacy

Cukup does not collect data from anyone. Since no data leaves the device, there are no age-based restrictions on data handling.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
