Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Last updated: May 2026

SliceStackX does not collect any personal data itself. Your scores, stats, and purchases are stored locally on your device and never transmitted anywhere by us.

What the App Stores Locally

The following data is saved on your device only and never uploaded:

  • High score and lifetime stack count
  • In-app purchase status (Remove Ads, The Gold Tower)
  • Game preferences and first-run state

Deleting the app removes all of this data permanently.

Advertising — Google AdMob

SliceStackX shows ads powered by Google AdMob for free players, including rewarded videos (for an extra revive) and occasional interstitials. AdMob may collect the following to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

You can limit ad tracking at any time via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads will still appear but will be less personalised.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchasing Remove Ads turns off all advertising entirely.

In-App Purchases

All purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. SliceStackX never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/privacy.

No Accounts, No Servers

SliceStackX has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend servers of our own. The game runs entirely on your device; the only network requests are to Google AdMob to fetch ads.

Children's Privacy

SliceStackX is not directed at children under 13. The app displays ads through Google AdMob. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
